FAQs

Common questions

How much will your services cost?

We agree a fixed fee before any work starts, based on the size and complexity of your affairs. Fees can be paid monthly by direct debit to spread the cost over the year.

Is it difficult to switch accountants?

No. Once you give us the go-ahead we contact your current accountant for a professional clearance letter and take over the records we need. Most switches are completed within a couple of weeks.

Do you work with clients outside your local area?

Yes. With cloud accounting software, secure document portals and video meetings, we work just as effectively with clients across the country.

Which accounting software do you support?

We work with all the major cloud packages, including Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage and FreeAgent, and can help you choose and set up the right one.

What information do you need to get started?

Your most recent accounts and tax returns, HMRC references (UTR, VAT and PAYE numbers where relevant) and proof of identity for our anti-money-laundering checks. We’ll send a simple checklist.
